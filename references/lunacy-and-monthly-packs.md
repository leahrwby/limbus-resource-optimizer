# Lunacy types and monthly supplies

Verified: 2026-08-04

## Currency types

- Free Lunacy and Paid Lunacy can both fund standard extractions and Enkephalin refills.
- Eligible mixed-currency spending prioritizes Free Lunacy before Paid Lunacy.
- Paid-only uses include the 13-Paid-Lunacy daily extraction, the 1300-Paid-Lunacy Limbus Pass, the 2700-Paid-Lunacy special Pass package (or 1700 after owning the Pass), 1300-Paid-Lunacy Announcers, and 2600-Paid-Lunacy prior-Pass E.G.O.
- Never add Free Lunacy to a Paid Lunacy budget for paid-only purchases.

## Monthly Lunacy Supply (large monthly card)

- Immediate reward: 650 Paid Lunacy.
- Daily reward: 65 Free Lunacy on login for 30 days.
- Full 30-login-day total: 650 Paid + 1950 Free = 2600 total Lunacy.

## Monthly Lunacy Batch (small monthly card)

- Immediate reward: 130 Paid Lunacy.
- Daily reward: 39 Free Lunacy on login for 30 days.
- Full 30-login-day total: 130 Paid + 1170 Free = 1300 total Lunacy.

## Owning both

- Immediate reward: 780 Paid Lunacy.
- Daily reward: 104 Free Lunacy.
- Full 30-login-day total: 780 Paid + 3120 Free = 3900 total Lunacy.
- The two products function separately.

## Duration and login rules

- Daily rewards arrive through in-game mail.
- A missed login day does not grant that day's daily reward.
- Repurchasing extends the reward duration rather than doubling the daily mail.
- The official FAQ says each product can be repurchased within its duration and extended up to 67 days. Ask for remaining claimable login days instead of assuming 30 when a pack is already active.
- Monetary price varies by platform and region; do not hard-code it into optimization.
